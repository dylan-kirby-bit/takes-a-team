MENTAL HEALTH MATTERS ARCADE — iPAD GAMES
Baylor University Counseling Center outreach

WHAT'S HERE
  index.html    Arcade home: staff pick today's game (IT's link opens this)
  team.html     Mental Health Takes a Team (Men's Mental Health Month)
  welcome.html  Your First Semester (New Student Welcome)
  sent.html     The page both Qualtrics results surveys redirect to (don't rename)
  sw.js         Keeps the arcade and both games working offline
  reader.html   Older results reader (no longer needed)
  qr-iphone.jpeg / qr-android.jpeg   The BUCC contact QR codes both games show

RESULTS
Each game saves results on the iPad and sends every finished game to its OWN
Qualtrics survey in the background:
  Takes a Team      -> "Takes a Team: Results drop"      (DROP_URL in team.html)
  Your First Semester -> "Welcome Game: Results drop"    (DROP_URL in welcome.html)
No names, emails, IDs or device information are collected.

AFTER EVERY EVENT
  1. Hold "Staff · hold" in the game you used.
  2. When it says "All games sent to Qualtrics ✓", tap "Erase all results".
  Backups on the Staff screen: Copy results, Export CSV file.

SWITCHING GAMES
Staff screen > "Arcade" returns to the home screen.

UPDATING
Upload changed files to GitHub (keep these exact names; rename any "(1)" downloads
first). Wait ~10 minutes, then on each iPad open the arcade online, close it fully,
and open it again. Never rename the repository: IT's web clip depends on it.
